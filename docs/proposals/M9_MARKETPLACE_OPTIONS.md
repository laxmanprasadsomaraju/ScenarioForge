## 3. Questions for Atlassian Developer / Marketplace Support

Get support: https://developer.atlassian.com/support. Ecosystem service desk (partner verification portal): https://ecosystem.atlassian.net/servicedesk/customer/portal/34/group/110/create/4219

1. Can an individual without a registered business sell a Paid via Atlassian Forge app? What should go in the registration and tax ID fields (for example a personal tax number such as an Indian PAN)?
2. How do payouts work for a non-US individual? Where is the W-8BEN submitted, is a foreign TIN on line 6a accepted, and does Article 7 apply?
3. How long does partner verification usually take once the forms are submitted?
4. Does the Gmail restriction apply to listing submissions, or only to Partner Portal access?
5. Can a Jira-only (single-app) Forge app call `requestConfluence` for Confluence pages on the same site? Does that pass review?
6. Are Global Apps (EAP) allowed on the Marketplace, free or paid, before 22 Dec 2026?
7. Can two of our apps (one Jira, one Confluence) share data, and if so, how?
8. What are the typical review times for a first paid Forge listing versus a free one?
9. (Codegeist team, via the community thread or the Devpost contact) For "Best Marketplace Published App", may the listed app be a different app id from the judged app that is shared by installation link? Does a pending approval ticket count, or must the listing be live by 22 Dec?

## 4. Recommended path

1. 12 Nov: the owner sends questions 1–8 to support and posts question 9 to the Codegeist organisers. Devpost cannot be read by automation (HTTP 403), so the owner reads the rules in a browser.
2. Use option **E** for the bonus: a separate free, Jira-only listed app id. It keeps the judged installation-link app unchanged, with Jira + Confluence. This is the compliant route with the lowest effort and deadline risk.
3. Upgrade to **A** only if support confirms that an individual can be paid out and verification will finish by about 1 Dec. Upgrade to **C** only if support confirms in writing that `requestConfluence` is allowed.
4. Stop rule (unchanged): if verification is blocked or unanswered by 27 Nov, choose **F**.
